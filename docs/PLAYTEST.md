# Human playtest

Start in Practice, then play a full 60-second run. Try the first direction sign with the starting aim. Hold as it approaches, wait for **Release now**, then let go. The next warning and speed signs are smaller. Later signs move off the default aim point and require a drag or a keyboard nudge.

| Check | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| First hit | Can you land a hit within three attempts using the prompts? |
| Timing | Can you tell whether a miss came from early release, late release, or aim? |
| Aiming | Can you keep the sign visible and aim comfortably on a phone in one hand? |
| Impact | Does the shatter and metallic hit feel satisfying? Compare the warning and speed signs with sound on. |
| Replay | Try a full run, then replay. Check the local best, accuracy, and highest streak on the result screen. |
| Interruption | Pause mid-hold, resume and check that the bottle count stays the same. |

If a throw feels wrong, note your device, orientation, the sign type and the feedback shown on release. The useful tuning decisions for the next pass are touch sensitivity, speed-sign timing and impact audio on real speakers.
